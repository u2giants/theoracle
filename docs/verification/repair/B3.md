## What the gate is

`.github/workflows/brain-quality.yml` ("Brain quality gate") runs nightly at
5:17 AM EST, after a successful `Verify live version` on main, and on
dispatch. It queues one eval on the deployed brain
(`POST /eval/run`, shared token), waits for the run, and applies
`B/eval/gate.py::decide`: **fail when the mean drops more than 0.05 from the
last accepted run or any question errors**. A pass is recorded as the next
baseline through the app's own `POST /eval/runs/{id}/accept`
(`cmap.eval_run.accepted`). A failure exits non-zero and opens-or-updates ONE
issue labelled `quality-regression` (advisory to merging only — owner ruling
2026-10-30).

## Why the gate is red, and what turns it green

The 30 B1 drafts are **unapproved** — the owner's approve pass on
`/map/eval` is the one human action in Phase B. The eval runner scores only
approved questions, so the eval refuses to run and the gate (correctly)
reports an error. After the owner approves, the next run (nightly 5:17 AM
EST or dispatch) scores the set; if it passes it becomes the `accepted`
baseline and the drop check arms for every run after that.
