# W10 independent original-source semantic acceptance protocol

Protocol owner: current #27 implementation owner. Written October 12, 2026 EDT.
This is a source-engineering protocol and read-only receipt validator, not an
accepted company corpus, a provider dispatch admission or a hosted release.
Source-only census by the extraction model remains diagnostic. The independent
assessor must separately accept the inventory against all complete originals as
well as the candidate; the census cannot grade its own sufficiency. No private
originals, source quotations, company answers or raw provider responses belong
in this public document or a technical-review packet.

## Exact roles and custody

1. The coordinator verifies original-register and qualified-reading hashes,
   current ownership, permissions and source revisions. Reuse qualified readings;
   do not regenerate originals. Freeze the complete original population and every
   section, table cell and diagram locator independently of candidate outputs.
   Region spans must cover every reading character, including whitespace. Region
   boundaries and counts come from retained original/parser receipts, never the
   generated graph. A missing parser locator is a census blocker.
2. A fresh source-only census context on the admitted reader
   `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` receives only those readings/locators and the criteria below.
   It cannot see any extraction, graph, candidate, report, old answer or grader
   feedback. Source instructions are data, not authority. Record its complete
   request/dispatch/raw-response hashes and locator accounting. Reuse an existing
   compatible source-only census only if its complete provenance proves this
   separation. Partition only at sealed source-region boundaries; retain a receipt
   for every interval. Freeze essential/detail and expected dispositions before
   candidate creation or exposure; timestamps alone do not establish blindness.
3. Build or reuse the separately sealed candidate using the normal new-core
   pipeline and exactly compatible source/schema/runtime/permission identities.
   Record actual creation/first-exposure time in independently reviewed provenance.
   A historical candidate created before this census is diagnostic only: do not
   relabel its timestamp. A fresh candidate needs normal extraction admission;
   this protocol does not authorize rereading or blind paid regeneration.
4. A separate actor/context on independently admitted `openai/gpt-6-astra`
   receives complete original readings/locators, the frozen diagnostic census,
   and the sealed candidate, not the implementer's desired verdict. It audits
   inventory sufficiency from the originals and then compares every obligation
   and substantive candidate assertion. It differs from the candidate-generating
   model and actor. Existing source-extraction/operating-audit approval does not
   admit this independent acceptance role. An omitted inventory obligation fails
   this assessment; it cannot quietly amend the frozen key to make output pass.
5. The coordinator reads the exact candidate from the current permission-checked
   ledger, derives its object-to-evidence map, and verifies it against the actual
   candidate object population. A provider-supplied coverage map is not trusted
   candidate evidence. A separate technical reviewer receives metadata and public
   synthetic examples only, validates protocol, context separation, actual
   dispatch/provenance receipts and exact receipt hashes, and issues the external
   semantic admission. The implementing actor cannot issue its own admission.
   The coordinator's trusted registry pins the exact admission hash outside the
   candidate packet. Review admission uses the existing assigned-reviewer gate;
   adding a hash to an untrusted packet grants nothing.
6. Invoke `verify_semantic_acceptance` with the admitted census/assessment,
   external trusted admission set, independent source-region register, current
   ledger candidate/evidence map, runtime/permission hashes and current-source/
   authorization callbacks. Deny access before retrieving a private reading and
   recheck before returning. Persist the full sealed result in approved private
   immutable evidence storage; public proof contains hashes and denominators only.
   This callable is not wired to production: the generic `private-audit` verifier
   still refuses without the separately admitted runtime/factory. No current
   hosted identity or private acceptance is inferred from synthetic calls.

## Assessor instructions and complete criteria

The source-only reader inventories source-explicit obligations with deterministic
source/revision/locator identities; it assigns essential/detail and
represented/unresolved expectations independently of output. Source-explicit
mandatory approvals, allowed branches, actors/handoffs, scope/date limits,
exceptions and material disagreements are essential. Explicit obligations must
be represented. Only genuine original ambiguity can expect unresolved, with
evidence, affected behavior denied/partial and an assigned disposition. No
essential item may be declared unsupported, inapplicable or quarantined to hide
an omission. Never fabricate quotes for absence.

The independent assessor returns one complete record for each of these seven
criteria, with original evidence and candidate IDs or a complete-inventory absence
reference. A unsupported or omitted essential assertion is a failing verdict.

- **Roles:** all explicit actors, responsibilities, approvals and handoffs remain;
  similarly named actors in different scopes are not silently merged.
- **Artifacts:** exact input/output/revision identities, repeated occurrences and
  source supports survive; aliases are not independent corroboration.
- **Interfaces:** cross-process connections have explicit original support or a
  current authorized mapping; no invented join, order or company-wide completeness.
- **Exceptions:** required branches, rework, rejection, joins and termination are
  represented with their own conditions; uncertain control remains partial.
- **Conflicts:** opposed claims retain both supports and overlapping scope;
  differing customer/date variants are not falsely called conflicts; no overwrites.
- **Gaps:** explicit unknowns and independently inferred missing slots stay visible;
  absence never becomes quoted policy, a made-up owner or a resolved fact.
- **Scope and unsupported claims:** every substantive candidate assertion, including
  completeness/causation and caveats, is covered by original evidence or labeled
  inference; zero unsupported facts, joins or privileged-source disclosure.

Every frozen obligation has exactly one disposition with evidence; essential
represented/unresolved denominators are reported separately. Detail gaps retain
their original IDs, reasons and named coordinator disposition in the protected
assessment record. Detail gaps cannot be hidden by a complete label. A failed
criterion, omitted inventory item or source-locator hole fails the artifact.
Any candidate correction changes its seal and requires a newly admitted
assessment. Keep the failed raw response privately; no tuning the frozen key to
the candidate. Same-model assessment is diagnostic-only and rejected as acceptance.

## Dispatch and release boundaries

All census/extraction/assessment calls must separately pass provider admission,
current model/pricing/context qualification and global reservation coverage.
Reuse the existing OpenRouter account, OpenAI-only route, no fallback,
data-collection deny, store false and existing retention terms; no new purchase,
provider, model substitution, agreement, key or rotation. Preserve $1/attempt,
$5/batch and $25/New York day across every account caller. Reserve before each
call. At most one rooted diagnosed affected-artifact correction; quota-only
failures are terminal. Missing global reservation coverage means no dispatch,
even when credit and an individual key's daily limit remain available.

Exact protocol/runtime, original-region register, census, candidate/evidence map,
assessment, permission, actors, candidate creation time, reviewer report and expiry
are bound in `SemanticAdmission`. That external admission must be currently trusted;
receipt hash fields alone do not prove review or real dispatch. The trusted
admission issuer checks actual provider model, raw response and cost receipts,
including no truncation, and independently rejects falsified context/time claims.
No placeholder admission or synthetic receipt may enter the private registry.

The validator proves independently admitted documentary evidence accounting only.
It explicitly denies business/actual-event/hosted acceptance, serving activation
and company generation. W11 still requires separately reviewed restricted hosting,
native runtime/worker identity, current source/access, backup-to-fresh-restore,
three admitted comparison runs or seven New York days and reversible cutover.
W12 still requires its admitted live call census and capability equivalents.
PM4Py solver qualification and genuine operational exports remain separate.
Company questions, reports, employee messages and Ilona/Jessica grading stay paused.
No S03–S16 outcome is accepted by this protocol's publication.
