## Fixed allocator and resource provenance

The trim threshold is the maximum unsigned64 value for this x86_64 variant, preventing automatic arena trimming from introducing forbidden brk; it is not a runtime measurement or permission to expand the syscall policy. Primary tunable range/threshold semantics: https://www.sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Tunables.html and https://www.sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Memory-Allocation-Tunables.html .
