## 5. Root causes and key findings

- **Routing bias root cause (the licensing-to-Albert bug):** `select_target` in [tick.py](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/interview/tick.py) used to rank people by summed edge weight to the question's entities; flow-chart lane headers like "Albert :)" accumulate the biggest weights. Worse, question `entity_ids` are ALL members of the question's area (not the people the question is about) and go **dangling** after entity re-resolution — any lookup keyed on them alone is unreliable; recover topic from question text via mention names too.
- **The fix shape that survived production:** manual `target_employee_id` → vetted `suggested_person` ∪ role-gated department matches (evidence-contributor preference within the pool) → **defer (None)** when the pool is exhausted. Never the all-employee evidence search, never the named-persons-by-weight rule, for vetted questions. The Person entity `The Oracle` is never a candidate.
- **Language root cause (the Russian/Vietnamese questions):** PR #483 (zh plan step 1.2, commit [`fb9b2aa2`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/commit/fb9b2aa2), merged 2026-11-10 8:35 AM EST) changed [community_report.md](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/prompts/community_report.md) to phrase open questions "in the dominant language of the cluster's quotes" — meant so Chinese material produced Chinese report prose — and `open_questions` was the one field with no English floor. The language detector also misreads short English as foreign. Timeline proof: 0 non-English rows before 2026-11-10 17:29 UTC; 63/78/233/106 per day after. Fixed in PR #581 (prompt English clause + `translate.ensure_english_question` store guard + `lang.looks_english`); zh send-time localization ([tick.py `_localized_question`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/interview/tick.py)) is untouched and stays.
- **`QUESTION_WINDOW_DAYS = 7`** ([inbound.py](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/interview/inbound.py)) — a person with an `asked` question inside 7 days is "busy" (one question in flight). `cmap.interview_send` is written ONLY by the tick (a direct proactive-route call records no send and does not consume quota); the weekly cap counts `interview_send` rows in the rolling last 7 days.
- **The weekly quota is a flood guard, not courtesy:** 2026-11-10 removing it for one tick sent ~1,700 messages company-wide. It counts conversation-starting questions only (owner-confirmed meaning, 2026-11-12).
- **Eval mean is answer-completeness, not correctness:** each question scores coverage of expected points + citations of expected sources; 0.4892 means "halfway to complete on these 15 questions", not "half the brain's conclusions are wrong". Two near-zero questions are abstention cases the grader penalises (see #520 series for grader work).
- **Laura's `departments` array vs `department` column:** production `public.employees` stores multi-department membership in the JSONB `departments` array; the tick matches `to_jsonb(e)->>'department'` (or the role-echoed array per PR #580). `employee_departments` org units are just a backfill of that array.
- **Interview answers become sources through the read pipeline:** `handle_inbound` writes the transcript to storage and queues `read_source` (see [test_interview.py `test_answer_becomes_source`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/tests/test_interview.py)). Hand-writing chunks+mentions bypasses embeddings and leaves a re-read landmine — always go through `read_source`.
- **Proactive Teams sends are NOT gated by the interview switch** (only `x-brain-token`); the switch gates the tick loop alone.
