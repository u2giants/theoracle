## 4. Everything we tried that did NOT work

- **Waiting on OpenAI `/v1/batches` tail items without stall-salvage OR breaking out of `cancelling` too early (`15s–30s`):** Submitting `100` `gpt-6-luna` `/v1/responses` items to `/v1/batches` (`batch_6b02f7a5cc2881908e403bc01b8d18d3`) completed `84/100` in ~90 seconds and then left the remaining `16/100` stuck in `in_progress` for >22 minutes; earlier large batches (`batch_6afd7c1062f081909378ae577f65bfee`, `1,249/1,250`) even expired at 24h waiting on 1 item! Worse, when `POST /v1/batches/{id}/cancel` is called, OpenAI keeps the batch in `status = "cancelling"` with `output_file_id = null` for ~3–5 minutes before transitioning to `"cancelled"` and populating `output_file_id`. Breaking out of `cancelling` after 15–30 seconds discards all `84%` already-completed (and already-billed) batch items! Setting `DEFAULT_STALL_SALVAGE_SECONDS = 180.0` and `DEFAULT_CANCEL_GRACE_SECONDS = 300.0` in [`llm_batch.py`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/llm_batch.py#L36-L37) harvests 100% of completed `output_file_id` rows and finishes only the small tail via `/v1/responses` `service_tier="flex"` (same 50% batch price).
- **Reordering `prompts/relations_messages*.md` for OpenAI automatic prefix caching:** OpenAI prefix caching requires `>= 1,024` identical leading tokens. Measuring all three templates (`relations_messages.md`: `3,857` fixed chars / ~964 tok, `371` before first slot; `relations_messages_audit.md`: `2,947` chars / ~737 tok; `relations_messages_gate.md`: `2,556` chars / ~639 tok) proved none reaches `1,024` tokens even if all fixed text is moved above `CONTEXT`/`PASSAGE`, and the theoretical ceiling across the entire 3,373-thread backlog is `< $0.46` while invalidating `message_relations_prompt_hash()`. Do not reorder or pad `relations_messages*.md`.
- **Combining `flat.classify_body` and `function_assign` in one ScaleDown `/classify` call, or adopting standalone ScaleDown `/classify` for `function_assign`:** Default `/classify` applies single-label softmax summing to `1.0` across all labels, so adding 13 business-function labels to the 6 passage-kind labels dilutes chatter scores below `CHATTER_SKIP_SCORE = 0.8`. Tested standalone across both `multi_label=false` and `multi_label=true` and thresholds `0.10..0.90` on `tuning_50`, ScaleDown `/classify` missed all three Step 2.5 gates on `heldout_30` (`23.88%` wrong picks, UB `30.43% > 10%`; `72.86%` recall, LB `66.18% < 85%`). Keep `BRAIN_SCALEDOWN_FUNCTION_ASSIGN_ENABLED=false` and use `openai/gpt-6-luna` for message `function_assign`.

## 5. Root causes and key findings

- **OpenAI `/v1/batches` + `/v1/responses` `service_tier="flex"` gives deterministic `<=30m` latency at exact 50% batch list pricing:** Both `/v1/batches` and `/v1/responses` (`service_tier="flex"`) bill `gpt-6-luna` at `$0.05/1M` input (`$0.005/1M` cached) and `$0.25/1M` output. Coalescing queued message jobs via `_claim_sibling_message_jobs` ([`relations.py:1481-1508`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/extract/relations.py#L1481-L1508)) runs up to 100 threads in 3 batch rounds total, while `salvage_with_flex=True` eliminates `/v1/batches` tail stalls without paying synchronous prices.
- **`GPT-6 Luna` beats both ScaleDown and `GLM-5.3 Flash` on `function_assign` precision AND cost:** On message passages, `GPT-6 Luna` cuts wrong business-function picks to `8.47%` (`5.49%` on `tuning_50`) vs `17.39%` (`GLM`) and `23.88%` (`ScaleDown`), lifts 15-question `brain/eval` end-to-end score to `0.7163` (`+0.0045` vs Step 2.3), and costs `$0.1704/1,000` passages sync (`$0.0852/1,000` batch) vs `$0.1966/1,000` on `GLM`.
- **Staged backlog recency ordering in `cmap.intake_thread`:** Production/staging `ingest._threads` ([`ingest.py:67-95`](https://github.com/u2giants/theoracle/blob/main/apps/brain/brain/intake/ingest.py#L67-L95)) orders `ORDER BY COALESCE(started_at, updated_at) ASC`.
