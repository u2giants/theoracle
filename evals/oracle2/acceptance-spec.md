# Oracle 2 acceptance set, S01

This public corpus is invented. It does not describe POP Creations policy. Freeze the 20 `A` questions and their answer keys before implementation comparisons. The ten `D` questions use different processes and source IDs and are available for development. The twelve `F` records are source parsing and adversarial fixtures, never business questions.

Run `python3 scripts/oracle2/validate_manifest.py --synthetic` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/oracle2 -p 'test_*.py'`. The validator enforces exact split/category counts, ID prefixes, acceptance-record checksums, a span for every declared source, nonempty answer fields, unique IDs, and disjoint processes, questions, spans and source IDs within and across splits. It compares acceptance content with the first committed manifest in full Git history; missing history or either baseline file fails the gate. A changed set needs a version bump and old/new comparison before quality claims. Checksums detect ordinary edits; Git history catches a coordinated same-version edit to data and checksum. Version 1 is anchored by the first commit that added the manifest, including after this PR merges. An evidence span is the invented source text at its locator; a valid citation must point to that span and support the asserted clause. `abstain` means decline the requested policy, approval or decision while stating any narrower facts the source actually establishes.

## Review procedure

For each answer, hide system identity from the reviewer and show only the question, permitted source spans, effective date/scope and output. Score fact correctness, mandatory exceptions, citation support, temporal and customer scope, usefulness and clarity separately as pass/fail. A required fact or exception missed is a failure. Any forbidden inference, fabricated approval, invented financial benefit, or cited span that does not support its clause is a failure. In an `abstain` case, a specific bounded gap and the supported partial facts are required. For improvement questions, distinguish observed evidence, hypothesis, test measure and missing inputs; a purchase or quantified ROI without supplied data fails. A reviewer records rationale and exact failed clause privately. A second reviewer resolves disputes before an aggregate score is published.

The S01 acceptance thresholds remain those in plan §10.3: at least 19/20 overall, category floors of 4/4, 4/4, 4/4, 3/3 and 4/5, and all five improvement cases meeting the mandatory recommendation contract. Source resolution is 100%; unsafe authority, evidence, access or financial errors fail regardless of average. Synthetic scores never establish business quality on real company material.
